Coral Reef is a skill-exchange platform where users can share skills they know, discover skills they want to learn and connect with compatible learning partners.

Your teaching skills and learning interests are compared with other users. Coral Reef calculates a compatibility score to help highlight suitable skill-exchange partners.

Yes. Coral Reef is designed around two-way knowledge exchange. You can list the skills you can teach and separately list the skills you want to learn.

Open Find Matches, select a compatible profile and use the connection option. Once a request is accepted, you can continue communicating with your learning partner.

Skill Verification provides a dedicated area where users can work toward demonstrating evidence of the skills they want to share.

The Learning Roadmap helps organize a learning goal into structured steps so that users can clearly understand what they want to learn and track their progress.

Coral Reef is currently designed as a college project demonstrating a skill-exchange platform and its core functionality.
